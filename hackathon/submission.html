Built from failures

Recovery behavior is part of the product.

  • A false-failed create is adopted when its on-chain position exists.
  • A false-successful stop is not considered closed until the chain agrees.
  • Unresolved executors and unreadable pools stay unclassified—never auto-cleaned as ghosts or phantoms.
  • Rate-limit responses cannot collapse scanner retry delay to zero.
  • A deterministic host adapter captures executor actions, waits for a later executor/chain/wallet reconciliation, and permits the learner to tune only bounded execution mechanics—never hard risk controls.
  • Restart-on-boot is enabled so an unattended run resumes its supervision loop.
